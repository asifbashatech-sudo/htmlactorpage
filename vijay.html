Vijay

Vijay is an Indin Tamil cinema. He has won numerous awards, including four Tamil Nadu State Film Awards, three Filmfare Awards South, and seven Vijay Awards. He is one of the highest-paid actors in Tamil cinema and has a significant fan following.

Vijay made his acting debut as a child artist in the film "Vetri" (1984) and later appeared in several films as a child actor. He made his debut as a lead actor in the film "Naalaiya Theerpu" (1992). Some of his notable films include "Ghilli" (2004), "Thuppakki" (2012), "Mersal" (2017), and "Master" (2021).
